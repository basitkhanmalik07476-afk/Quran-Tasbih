The 30 Juz (Sipara) starting points are from AlQuran Cloud Quran metadata:
https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/meta

Quran recitation audio is streamed on demand from the AlQuran Cloud CDN
(islamic.network), using Mishary Rashid Alafasy's Murattal recitation:
https://cdn.islamic.network/quran/audio-surah/128/ar.alafasy/

The bundled Quran text contains 6,236 ayah entries in the widely used Kufan
verse-numbering count. Other traditional verse-counting systems differ in
some verse divisions. The often-quoted number 6,666 is a popular figure; this
app follows the 6,236-entry count and does not omit Quran text.

Translations are interpretations of the meanings and are not a replacement
for the Arabic Quran. The translation text is not modified in this app.
